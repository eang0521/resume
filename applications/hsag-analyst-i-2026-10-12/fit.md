## Gaps and how to handle them
- **SAS:** Not claimed anywhere. If asked, say you are eager and quick to learn it, and point to Python/R/SQL as transferable. Optionally spend a few hours on a free SAS tutorial before an interview.
- **Healthcare data:** Lean on study-design coursework (ANOVA, power and sample size, bootstrapping) and your public-data project. Consider a small public health dataset project (e.g. CDC or CMS open data) to close this gap.
