## Overview
Entry-level role intended to develop early-career talent. Embedded in Customer Success, owning data quality for commercial customers of Fortress's C-SCRM (Cyber Supply Chain Risk Management) platform. Customers are in sectors like energy, utilities, and manufacturing. Pipeline to permanent employment for strong performers.

## Responsibilities
- Validate, reconcile, and standardize customer-provided supplier, product, and vendor data for accuracy and completeness in the C-SCRM platform
- Support customer onboarding: ingest, normalize, and baseline new data sources with Customer Success Managers to meet implementation timelines
- Identify, analyze, and resolve data anomalies and inconsistencies affecting customer-facing risk outputs
- Develop and run data validation workflows and queries using Excel, Looker, SQL, or scripting tools
- Track, document, and manage data defects and remediation in Jira; communicate status to internal teams and customers
- Join customer working sessions and QBR preparation; improve documentation and processes
- Other duties as assigned

## Minimum qualifications
- 0–1 year of experience in data quality, data analysis, cybersecurity, or related technical field (internship or academic project experience welcome)
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel for data analysis and validation
- Familiarity with or willingness to learn query or scripting tools (e.g., SQL, Python)
- Experience using Jira or similar ticketing systems, or demonstrated ability to quickly learn workflow tools
- Ability to work in a client-facing or cross-functional environment and communicate data issues clearly to non-technical audiences
- Strong analytical judgment, attention to detail, ability to work independently with guidance
- Ability to use AI tools through basic prompting for drafting, summarizing, brainstorming, while applying judgment to AI output

## Preferred skills
- Coursework or project experience in data analysis, cybersecurity, supply chain, or risk management
- Familiarity with Supply Chain Risk Management (SCRM) or third-party risk concepts
- Data visualization tools such as Looker, Tableau, or Power BI
- Interest in or exposure to commercial critical infrastructure sectors (energy, utilities, manufacturing)
