## Gaps and how to address them honestly
- **Program management knowledge:** Do not claim it. In an interview, frame your work as "managing a project end to end": scoping features with a founder, assessing delay risks for a client, directing a multi-part project. If you want a real credential, the free Google Project Management or CAPM prep material would let you honestly say you studied PM basics (not on your profile today).
- **Vendors, budgets, policies:** Not claimed. The Berkeley dashboards can be used to say you have worked with budget data, but you have not owned a budget; do not imply it.
- **Access and Visio:** Both are quick to learn; consider a short Visio/Access tutorial before an interview so you can honestly say you have started.
- **Utility context:** Read PG&E's public material on Energy Delivery (the business unit named in the posting) before an interview. Your Concord residency and customer relationship are real and are used in the cover letter.
- **Data-heavy resume vs. PM role:** Your strongest material is analytic. The resume leads with client work, alerts, and communication to read as program-support rather than data-science.
