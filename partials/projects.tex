\section*{Projects}
\textbf{Contra Costa County Housing AVM} --- Built an end-to-end data pipeline that scrapes, cleans, and consolidates 3,000+ home listings from Realtor.com, then loads them into a predictive model of Contra Costa County home values, achieving a model that explains more than 86\% of the variance in home prices.\\[4pt]
\textbf{NBA Game Simulator} --- Built a Python pipeline that scrapes real NBA player and team stats spanning the last 40 seasons to drive a dice-based basketball game simulation, capable of simulating both individual games and full seasons with play-by-play logs and final box scores.
